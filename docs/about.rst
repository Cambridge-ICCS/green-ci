About
=====

The ``green-ci`` tool was created in a hackathon at the `Virtual Institute for
Scientific Software <https://www.schmidtsciences.org/viss/>`__ Convening in
Cambridge, UK, in September 2025. Its initial and ongoing development has been
done by software engineers from the `Institute of Computing for Climate Science
<https://iccs.cam.ac.uk/>`__ at the Unversity of Cambridge, UK, and the
`Scientific Software Engineering Center
<https://escience.washington.edu/software-engineering/ssec/>`__ at the
University of Washington, USA.

Development of the ``green-ci`` tool is supported by `Schmidt Sciences, LLC
<https://www.schmidtsciences.org/>`__.
